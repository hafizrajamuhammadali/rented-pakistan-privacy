Rented Pakistan allows registered users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

Important: Account deletion is permanent and may not be reversible. Some information may be retained where required by law, for security, fraud prevention, or legitimate business purposes.

How to Delete Your Account

If you have an account in the Rented Pakistan app, you can request account deletion using the following methods:

  1. Open the Rented Pakistan app.
  2. Sign in to your account.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Follow the instructions shown in the app to confirm account deletion.

Unable to Access Your Account?

If you cannot access your account or cannot use the in-app account deletion option, you can submit a deletion request by contacting us using the email address below.

Request Account Deletion

What Data Will Be Deleted?

When your account deletion request is processed, we will delete or remove the personal information associated with your account where applicable, including account information and associated user data that is no longer required.

Property listings and other content associated with your account may also be removed where applicable.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may need to be retained for a limited period when required by applicable law, legal obligations, security requirements, fraud prevention, dispute resolution, or other legitimate purposes.
