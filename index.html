Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Rented Pakistan ("we", "our", "us", "the App") is a property rental listing platform that allows property owners in Pakistan to list houses/properties for rent, and allows users looking for rental properties to browse listings and contact owners directly. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect information when you use our mobile application.

By downloading, installing, or using Rented Pakistan, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the App.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information Provided by Property Owners

  • Account information: name, email address, and/or phone number used to create an account and log in.
  • Contact number: the mobile/cell number you provide, which is displayed publicly on your listing so interested renters can contact you directly.
  • Property location: the address/location you select or enter for your listed property, including map coordinates where applicable.
  • Property images: photographs of the property that you upload and choose to make publicly visible on your listing.
  • Additional property details such as price, description, rooms, washrooms, kitchen, porch, portion, and other information you choose to provide.

1.2 Information from Users Browsing Listings

Users can browse property listings, view locations, and see owner contact numbers without creating an account or logging in. For these users, we may automatically collect limited technical information as described below.

1.3 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device information, such as device model, operating system, unique device identifiers, and app version.
  • Log data, including IP address, date/time of access, crash reports, and diagnostic information.
  • Approximate or precise location data if location permission is granted, used to show nearby rental properties or help owners set their property location.
  • Advertising identifiers such as Google Advertising ID / AAID, used for showing and measuring advertisements.

2. Permissions Used by the App

Depending on your device and how you use the App, we may request the following permissions:

  • Location: to detect your current location, show nearby rental properties, or allow owners to set the location of a property.
  • Camera / Photo Gallery / Storage: to allow property owners to take or select photos of their property for uploading.
  • Internet / Network Access: required to load listings, submit data, and display advertisements.
  • Phone: if applicable, to allow the Call button to open the phone dialer with the owner's number pre-filled.

You may deny or revoke these permissions at any time through your device settings. However, some features may not function properly without them.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage property owner accounts.
  • To publish property listings including location, images, and contact number.
  • To allow renters to view property details and contact owners.
  • To operate, maintain, and improve the App.
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues, fraud, or misuse.
  • To display advertisements and measure advertising performance.
  • To communicate with users regarding support requests.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

Rented Pakistan uses, or may in the future use, third-party services to display advertisements and analyze app usage, including Google AdMob and related Google services such as Firebase and Google Analytics for Firebase.

These third parties may collect and use information such as advertising ID, device information, approximate location, and app usage data to show personalized or non-personalized advertisements and measure advertising performance.

Google's use of advertising data is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy and related policies.

More information: Google Partner Sites Policy

You may manage personalized advertising preferences through your Android device settings or Google's advertising settings.

Google Ad Settings

We may also use other third-party service providers such as cloud hosting, databases, analytics, and crash-reporting services to operate the App. These providers process information as necessary to provide their services.

5. Public Nature of Listing Information

When a property owner creates a listing, certain information is intentionally made public and visible to users of the App, including users who are not logged in.

This may include property location, property images, and the owner's contact number. This is a core function of the App, allowing renters to view properties and directly contact owners.

Property owners should only submit information they are comfortable sharing publicly.

6. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Publicly as part of a property listing, including location, images, and contact number.
  • With service providers who help us operate the App, including hosting, analytics, and advertising providers.
  • If required by law, legal process, or governmental request.
  • To protect the rights, property, or safety of the App, our users, or the public.
  • In connection with a business transfer, merger, or acquisition, where applicable.

7. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Data Retention

We retain account and listing information for as long as your account or listing remains active, or as needed to provide our services.

You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

Rented Pakistan is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or the minimum age required in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

10. Your Rights and Choices

  • You may access, update, or correct your account and listing information through the App where applicable.
  • You may request deletion of your account and personal data by contacting us.
  • You may disable location, camera, or storage permissions through your device settings.
  • You may manage personalized advertising preferences through your device settings.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

Continued use of the App after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
